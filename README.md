# ॐ OM SHAKTHI Catering - Official Promotional Website

A modern, high-conversion, mobile-responsive business promotion website crafted specifically for **OM SHAKTHI Catering** (Wedding, Reception & Traditional Event Feasts).

---

## 🌟 Key Features

1. **Rich Hero Showcase**:
   - High-impact visual hero banner with trust metrics (25+ Years Legacy, 1,500+ Events, 100% Pure & Hygienic, 4.9★ Rating).
   - Direct Call-to-Actions (Instant Estimate, Explore Menus, WhatsApp Booking).

2. **Founder & Tradition Spotlight**:
   - Highlight of founder **Sri N.G. Bhoopathy** and master caterers.
   - Core pillars: Authentic Recipes, 5-Star Cleanliness (100% RO Water), Royal Presentation, and Master Supervision.

3. **Core Catering Services**:
   - Grand Wedding & Muhurtham Leaf Feasts (Elai Sapadu with 24+ items).
   - Royal Reception Buffets (Golden chafing warmers & multi-cuisine spread).
   - Sizzling Live Counters (Crispy Ghee Podi Dosa, Tawa Parotta, Delhi Chaat).
   - Luxury Designer Ice Cream Parlour & Exotic Fruit Carving Islands.
   - Auspicious Family Events (Gruhapravesam, Seemantham, Upanayanam, Birthdays).
   - Outdoor Lawns & Corporate Banquets.

4. **Interactive Menu Explorer (Tabbed)**:
   - Traditional Muhurtham Leaf Feast (Starters, Sweets, Rice, Sambar/Rasam, Curries, Accompaniments, Desserts).
   - Royal Reception Buffet (Multi-cuisine, Tandoor Rotis, Biryani, Live Jalebi/Rabdi).
   - Live Stalls & Street Chaat Bazaar (10+ Dosa varieties, Pani Puri, Mocktails, Jalebi).
   - Auspicious Breakfast & Tiffin (Ven Pongal, Idli, Vadai, Poori, Kumbakonam Degree Coffee).

5. **Instant Catering Budget & Cost Estimator**:
   - Interactive Guest Count Slider (50 to 3,000+ guests).
   - Package selection (Muhurtham Leaf Feast, Reception Buffet, VIP Banquet, Breakfast).
   - Add-on stalls (Live Dosa, Ice Cream Island, Mocktail Bar, Chaat).
   - Dynamic real-time calculation of Per-Plate and Total Estimated Budget.
   - **One-click "Book with this Quote on WhatsApp"** button that pre-formats all details!

6. **Real Event Photo Gallery with Interactive Lightbox**:
   - Powered by real event photography from `Desktop\NGB`.
   - Category filtering (All, Royal Buffets, Live Cooking, Hall Setups, Feasting & Guests).
   - Full-screen Lightbox with captions, zoom, and next/previous navigation.

7. **Instant Inquiries & WhatsApp Integration**:
   - Quick Booking Form with immediate WhatsApp pre-filled dispatch.
   - Floating WhatsApp button and mobile sticky "Call Directly" & "WhatsApp Us" bar.
   - Comprehensive contact details, FAQs accordion, and structured schema.org markup for local Google SEO.
