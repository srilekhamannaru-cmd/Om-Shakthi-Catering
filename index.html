About OM SHAKTHI Catering

A Sacred Tradition of Uncompromising Taste & Royal Hospitality

Headquartered in Rajanagaram, Pichatur (near Tirupati, Andhra Pradesh) under the experienced vision of Sri N.G. Bhoopathy, OM SHAKTHI Catering is renowned across Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, and Telangana. We cater for all auspicious events with mastery across all cuisines—from authentic Andhra delicacies and traditional Tamil Kalyana Seer feasts to royal North Indian spreads, continental specialties, and grand multi-cuisine buffets. We believe that feeding guests during sacred milestones—weddings, housewarmings, and family gatherings—is not merely an event, but a sacred duty performed with love, dedication, and culinary reverence.

Every dish we prepare combines traditional recipes passed down through master cooks, pure cold-pressed oils, freshly ground aromatics, farm-fresh vegetables, and premium dairy ingredients. From a 50-guest intimate home puja to a 5,000-guest grand wedding reception, our head masters personally inspect every single detail.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about booking and planning catering with OM SHAKTHI.

How far in advance should we book OM SHAKTHI Catering?

For peak wedding seasons (Muhurtham dates), we recommend booking 2 to 4 months in advance to reserve your preferred date. However, for smaller family gatherings or corporate events, we can accommodate bookings with 1 to 2 weeks notice depending on slot availability.

Do you cater across different cities and outdoor venues?

Yes! Based in Rajanagaram, Pichatur (near Tirupati, AP), OM SHAKTHI Catering serves extensively across Andhra Pradesh, Tamil Nadu, and Telangana. We cater at marriage halls, convention centres, temple mandapams, outdoor lawns, and private residences.

Can we arrange a food tasting session before confirming the booking?

Absolutely! We welcome prospective wedding clients to sample our signature dishes, sweets, and gravies. Simply reach out via phone or WhatsApp, and our coordinator will schedule a tasting session for your family.

Do you provide full setup including tables, warmers, and servers?

Yes, our service is 100% turnkey. We provide the complete setup: royal golden chafing dishes, designer illuminated stalls, serving vessels, purified drinking water dispensers, uniformed servers, supervisors, and post-service waste management.
